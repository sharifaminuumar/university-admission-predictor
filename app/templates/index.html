How it works

Three steps, no account, no payment.

1

Input WASSCE Grades

Enter your four core subjects and three electives. Nothing is stored, and no sign-up is required.

2

Algorithmic Evaluation

The engine checks mandatory cores, "any N of these" elective pools, D7 diploma pathways, and your margin against each cut-off.

3

Actionable Insights

Results come banded Safe, Competitive, Reach or Near Miss — with a "what-if" tip showing the single grade upgrade that would close the gap.

warning An important limitation

Most Ghanaian universities do not publish per-programme cut-offs. Where none exists we fall back to the institution's site-wide minimum entry aggregate and label it General entry ceiling. Clearing it means you meet the minimum requirement — not that you would be admitted. Several programmes also require entrance exams or interviews that a grade-based tool cannot model.

Verification & Benchmarking

Programme requirements and cut-offs are sourced from official university admission portals, prospectuses and published gazettes, then cross-referenced across those official sources.

Where an institution publishes no per-programme cut-off, the card says so rather than showing an invented number. Unsourced third-party figures are never used.
