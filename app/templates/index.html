About EduPredict Ghana

Built so no Ghanaian student has to pay to find out where they stand.

volunteer_activism

100% Free

No paid checks, no credits, no account. Ghanaian students already pay enough for application forms.

lock

Privacy-First

Your grades are never stored. Predictions are computed per request, and a shared link only contains what you chose to put in it.

lightbulb

Goal Advice

When you narrowly miss a programme, we compute the single grade upgrade that would close the gap — not just a pass/fail verdict.

fact_check

Honest About Data

Every cut-off is labelled by how much it can be trusted. We never present a university's minimum entry bar as if it were a competitive cut-off.

warning An important limitation

Most Ghanaian universities do not publish per-programme cut-offs. Where none exists we fall back to the institution's site-wide minimum entry aggregate and label it General entry ceiling. Clearing it means you meet the minimum requirement — not that you would be admitted. Several programmes also require entrance exams or interviews that a grade-based tool cannot model.
